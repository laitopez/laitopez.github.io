## An overview of Kelly Dr, the SRT, and Girard Bridge

---

My amateur traffic glow up of a much needed connection between the Schuylkill River Trail and Girard bridge.

_Disclaimer: I am not a traffic engineer or experienced in the things I'm about to talk about. Just a frustrated citizen and nearby resident asking for overdue improvements._

[Here](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4fKMMqRHRbewnP3w9) is the proposed location if you are not familiar.

![srt-new-access](/srt-new-access.png)

In my view it is criminal that there is no official access to the city from this part of the SRT. Kelly Dr is managed by PennDOT rather than the city and, from what I understand, getting the state agency to make any improvements other than _maximum car throughput_ is a nearly impossible task. I would guess this is why the necessary crossing has been missing here for so long...

### Lets take a look at the current conditions

1. From the top of Brewery Hill Dr if you [take a look](https://www.google.com/maps/@39.9755332,-75.1903359,23a,90y,331.9h,84.62t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1shO7kEelBnEEvSA34_R-uUw!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.clients6.google.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fcb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile%26w%3D900%26h%3D600%26pitch%3D5.382790372761349%26panoid%3DhO7kEelBnEEvSA34_R-uUw%26yaw%3D331.89903254992146!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAxOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D) at google maps you'll find a section of the road/crosswalk was narrowed in anticipation of the 2026 FIFA fanfest. Despite this change there is still plenty of room for improvements.... The car in the picture has more than enough space. Also, I would not want to be caught in the middle of this cross walk as a pedestrian. I pulled the crosswalk data for the entire city and this intersection is in the top 10 longest crossings.

![brewery-dr-buffer-zone](/brewery-dr-buffer-zone.png)

Replacing the roads existing dead space to widen the path here would help with all of these issues. At the base of Brewery Hill Dr you can find a staircase on the other side of Girard Bridge that takes you down to Kelly Dr. In June 2026 Lemon Hill Neighbors did great work restoring this staircase - you can find a post on their [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZQbdKsiVal/?img_index=1). ### Existing proposals

It looks like there have been two design proposal so far for improving this space.

1. The [Philadelphia Trail Master Plan](https://www.phila.gov/media/20210707101708/2021_TrailPlan_Update.7.1.pdf) seems to have something listed as "Girard Avenue Bridge Sidepath" as far back as 2014. However, its not clear from any of the documents or maps if this is a path along Girard bridge parallel with car traffic _or_ is about creating a connection to the SRT underneath the bridge.
2. A 2024 PennPraxis redesign you can find [here](https://www.design.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/fairmount_park_final_report.pdf) on page 38 lists ambitious plans and renderings for bridging this connection between Brewerytown and the trail among other improvements.

![praxis-rendering](/praxis-rendering.jpg)

### Conclusion

This area looks like its begging to have everything connected - Lemon Hill, Girard Bride, SRT, and the Glendinning Rock Garden park. Below are the requests.

1. Request a traffic light and crosswalk at Girard bridge.
2. Restructure Brewery Hill Dr to:
   - Replace dead road space with a multi use path from the top of Girard bridge to Kelly Dr
   - Connect Brewery Hill Dr path to the SRT with the new crossing
   - Improve access to the Glendinning Rock Garden park along multi use path
3. Or... just remove Brewery Hill Dr as a car road all together and make it a trail with access to the SRT?
4. Potentially create a "Friends of Glendinning Rock Garden Park" group in order to establish some legitimacy in this area and to coalesce nearby residents and orgs.
